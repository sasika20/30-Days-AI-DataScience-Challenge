# Day 1 - Statistics Basics

## What is Statistics?
Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data.

## Population
The complete set of data or observations.

Example:
All students in a college.

## Sample
A subset of the population.

Example:
100 students selected from the college.

## Mean
Average of all values.

Formula:
Mean = Sum of Observations / Number of Observations

## Median
Middle value of an ordered dataset.

## Mode
Most frequently occurring value in a dataset.

## Key Takeaways
- Population contains all observations.
- Sample is a subset of the population.
- Mean, Median, and Mode are measures of central tendency.
